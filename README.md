# CivicPro

Open-source statutory bookkeeping software for small businesses and sole traders. You enter a business transaction as a single line; the program generates the balanced double-entry journal entry behind it, checks it, and produces the trial balance, balance sheet, income statement and statutory ledgers.

- Runs on a single computer (SQLite) or on a server in your local network (PostgreSQL), on Windows, macOS and Linux. Your data stays with you; there is no telemetry.
- Country rules live in separate country packs, outside the core.
- Includes counterparty accounts, due dates and instalments, cheques and promissory notes, loans and project tracking. Invoicing, inventory quantities, payroll, tax filing and e-documents are out of scope.
